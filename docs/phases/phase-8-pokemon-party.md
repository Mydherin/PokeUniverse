# Phase 8: Pokémon and Party

**Goal:** players manage their Pokémon, and their lead Pokémon follows them.

The Pokémon data model, the party, the party screen and the summary moved to [Phase 4](phase-4-players-room.md) with the battles ([plans/battles.md](../plans/battles.md)).

## Scope

- Bill's PC boxes added to the PC menu from [Phase 4](phase-4-players-room.md).
- Field moves from the party screen, TM/HM teaching (TM and tutor learnsets), held-item effects outside battle.
- **Following Pokémon** with HGSS-style overworld sprites (`pokeemerald-expansion`'s `overworld.png` for every species), visible to other players.

## Done When

- A player manages their team and boxes, and their lead Pokémon follows them in the world.
