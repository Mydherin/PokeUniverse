# Phase 5: Pokémon and Party

**Goal:** players own Pokémon, and their lead Pokémon follows them.

## Scope

- Data model for player Pokémon: species, stats, moves, level, experience.
- Choosing a starter.
- Party and PC storage.
- **Following Pokémon** with HGSS-style overworld sprites, visible to other players.

## Done When

- A player has a team, manages it, and their lead Pokémon follows them in the world.
