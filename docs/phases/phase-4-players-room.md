# Phase 4: Player's Room

**Goal:** the player appears in their room, walks with every move decided by the server, and uses the START menu and their PC like in FireRed.

## Scope

- ✅ Create `tool-maps` map commands (Go CLI): `convert`, `validate`, `compile`, `tilesets`, `preview`, `catalog-preview`, `rename` ([maps.md](../maps.md)); tested on the player's room.
- Convert FireRed's maps into the text format, with FireRed's tilesets and their tile catalogs. ✅ the room (2F); the first floor is next.
- ✅ Phaser renders compiled maps (pixel-perfect) under the GBA screen, camera placed as in FireRed. CONTINUE and the end of New Game fade in from black into the trainer's map, with its music; `mic-core` keeps where the trainer is (New Game: the room, tile 6, 6, facing north). The camera follows the player.
- ✅ Player sprite (boy or girl) with walk and run animations; turning in place and bumping (with its sound) as in FireRed. Running follows the original: the room doesn't allow it, and the Running Shoes come later (story flags, Phase 5).
- ✅ Server-authoritative grid movement and collisions (walls, one-way tiles, elevation, stair tiles): the client predicts, `mic-core` checks every step and pace, and corrects ([Movement](../tech-stack.md#movement)).
- ✅ Objects in the room can be checked (A) and answer with the text box: signs, the NES, the PC (boot message), furniture by tile behavior; walking up to a signpost reads it.
- ✅ **START menu** like FireRed, with the entries it shows at this point: BAG, the player's name, SAVE, OPTION, EXIT (POKéDEX and POKéMON appear later, by story flags). Help bar for the selected entry, cursor (remembered), sounds and closing as in the original; the player can't move while it's open. Screens it opens fade out of the world and back into it with the menu open.
  - ✅ **BAG:** ITEMS, KEY ITEMS and POKé BALLS pockets with switching (LEFT/RIGHT), the scrolling list with CANCEL, item description and icon (loaded on demand), quantities, the shaking bag, red arrows, opening and closing curtain, remembered pocket and cursor. A opens the item's menu as FireRed: USE/CHECK/OPEN, GIVE, TOSS (how many, then YES/NO), REGISTER/DESELECT (the SELECT mark in the list), CANCEL; SELECT moves an item within its pocket (the swap line). `mic-core` decides every action and saves it before answering; the bag shows the outcome with FireRed's texts and sounds.
  - ✅ **Item effects** (FireRed's field use functions, as a strategy per use in `mic-core`): with no Pokémon yet, party items say "There is no POKéMON."; GIVE: important items "can't be held", others no Pokémon; "This isn't the time to use that!" (OAK) where FireRed says it (bike indoors, rod not facing water, ESCAPE ROPE where escaping isn't allowed, VS SEEKER indoors, key items with no use); REPEL, SUPER and MAX REPEL are used up and keep their steps (the step count comes with encounters); a REPEL still in effect "lingers"; COIN CASE and POWDER JAR show the coins and powder; POKé FLUTE plays its catchy tune; WHITE/BLACK FLUTE set the lure or repel (needs a Pokémon); ITEMFINDER closes the bag and answers on the field. SELECT on the field uses the registered item (or explains registering).
  - ✅ **TM CASE and BERRY POUCH:** opened with OPEN from the BAG (back into the BAG) or SELECT on the field when registered (back to the field; SELECT also closes them), fading in and out. TM CASE: HMs then TMs with their move (No. in the small font, an HM's mark), a TM's quantity, the disc rising out of the case tinted by the move's type, the move's type, power, accuracy and PP (FireRed's move data from `make text` until the moves catalog, [Phase 9](phase-9-battles.md)) and the description; USE, GIVE, EXIT. BERRY POUCH: berries by number with quantities, the wobbling pouch, icon and description; USE, GIVE, TOSS (how many, then YES/NO), EXIT. Both sort their pocket on screen as FireRed does, remember the cursor, and show `mic-core`'s answers with FireRed's texts: "There is no POKéMON.", an HM "can't be held", OAK's "not the time" for berries without a field use.
  - Next for items: the screens items open (TOWN MAP, FAME CHECKER, TEACHY TV, reading mail); field effects (riding, fishing, ESCAPE ROPE, VS SEEKER, the ITEMFINDER's arrows) with the outdoor maps; effects on Pokémon with the party ([Phase 8](phase-8-pokemon-party.md)).
  - ✅ **Player (Trainer Card):** name, ID No., money, play time and badges, front and back with the flip, boy or girl version. Each New Game gets a random trainer ID (ID No. = its low 16 bits) and ¥3000, saved in PostgreSQL. Badges, stars and the back's records appear with what earns them. Play time counts while the trainer is in the world: `mic-core` counts real time and saves it with the position; the client counts on frame by frame from each world snapshot.
  - **SAVE:** FireRed's dialog with the save stats (player, badges, Pokédex, time) and YES/NO; YES makes `mic-core` save the trainer to PostgreSQL right away.
  - ✅ **OPTION:** FireRed's screen: text speed, battle scene, battle style, sound, button mode and frame (TYPE1–10), UP/DOWN to pick, LEFT/RIGHT to switch, the picked row brighter, no sounds; the new frame shows at once, A or B closes it. Saved per trainer in PostgreSQL (`PUT /api/trainer/options`); New Game resets them to FireRed's defaults. Text speed applies to the message box, frame to every standard window (START menu, main menu, messages), sound (mono or stereo) to all audio, button mode to the START menu's help bar and L=A; battle options are kept for [Phase 9](phase-9-battles.md).
- **Bedroom PC** after the boot message: ITEM STORAGE (WITHDRAW ITEM, DEPOSIT ITEM, TOSS ITEM), MAILBOX (empty) and TURN OFF, with FireRed's screens, texts and sounds. It starts with one POTION, like the original.
- ✅ **Items:** every FireRed item (307) in one catalog in `lib-contracts` with its rules (pocket, price, important, registrable, field use, needs a Pokémon); the bag's five pockets (ITEMS, KEY ITEMS, POKé BALLS, TM CASE, BERRY POUCH; one slot per item, so one of each always fits), the registered item and what items keep (REPEL steps, flute, coins, berry powder) per trainer in PostgreSQL; `mic-core` decides every change. For testing, New Game (and a migration, for trainers that existed) gives 5 of every item and 1 of each important one. Checked in the game: every item, TM, HM and berry shows in its pocket or screen (a quick check, not every action of every item).
- Warps between maps: the stairs lead down to the house's first floor.
- ✅ Position saved to Redis every tick and copied to PostgreSQL; after a crash the player continues on the same tile. One copy of the game plays a trainer at a time: CONTINUE (or New Game) in a new copy takes it over, and the old copy stops with a dialog that only goes back home.
- Keyboard controls (desktop) and touch controls (mobile).

## Done When

- The AI edits a text map, validates and compiles it, and checks the result as a PNG.
- After Oak's speech, the player walks around their room and down the stairs on desktop and mobile.
- In the room, every START menu entry and the PC work like in FireRed: withdraw the POTION, see it in the BAG, deposit or toss it; change the options and see them applied; save; read the Trainer Card.
- Items, money and options survive a reload and a `mic-core` restart.
- Restarting `mic-core` mid-walk puts the player back on their last tile.
