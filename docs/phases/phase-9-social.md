# Phase 9: Social

**Goal:** players interact with each other beyond seeing and chatting.

## Scope

- **Social menus:** friends list, and the menu to act on another player (trade, battle).
- Trading Pokémon between players, saved in one transaction (both Pokémon move or neither does).
- PvP battles between players.

## Done When

- Two players find each other in the social menus, trade Pokémon and battle each other.
