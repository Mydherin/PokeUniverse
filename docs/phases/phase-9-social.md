# Phase 9: Social

**Goal:** players interact with each other beyond seeing and chatting.

## Scope

- **Trainer card on A:** pressing A facing another player shows their trainer card with their name (user 2026-11-10; no names over sprites, [Phase 8 D11](phase-8-multiplayer-world/milestones/context.md#decisions)); from it, act on them (trade, battle).
- **Social menu:** a global friends list (across zones and replicas), adding friends, a player's busy state (in battle, Phase 8 D9).
- **Private chat:** one channel per friend.
- **Parties:** create a party, invite players; group chat per party.
- **From zone chat** (Phase 8's general chat, D13): pick a line to add its player as a friend or invite them to a party.
- Trading Pokémon between players, saved in one transaction (both Pokémon move or neither does).
- PvP battles between players.

## Done When

- Two players find each other in the social menus, trade Pokémon and battle each other.
- Friends chat privately and a party chats as a group, across replicas; pressing A on a player shows their trainer card.
