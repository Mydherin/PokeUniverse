## How to Write a Behavior

1. **Find it:** `references/pokefirered/src/battle_anim_<file>.c`. Don't read whole files: `grep -n` the name and read the template, its anim tables, its callback and `_Step` functions. Files of 1000+ lines cost a lot of context; `effects_*` are 4000-5600.
2. **Translate the template:** `.tileTag` → `tag: "ANIM_TAG_X"`; `.oam` → `width`, `height`, `ObjBlend` → `blend: true`, `AffineNormal` → `affineNormal: true`, `AffineDouble` → `affine: true`; `.anims`/`.affineAnims` → tables with `F`, `A`, `J`, `L`, `END` (util.ts). **`.anims = &table[n]` starts at that entry: `.slice(n)`.** Tags are already in `art.json`.
3. **Write it from zero** under the game's name in the family's file (`behaviors/<family>.ts`, exporting `<FAMILY>_TEMPLATES`, `_TASKS`), and spread new exports in `behaviors.ts`. Shared effects: `effects-1.ts`, `effects-2.ts`, `effects-3.ts` (`effects.ts` holds the earlier ones and `monAffine`); `utility.ts` (utility_funcs, mons, smokescreen); `general.ts` (general animations, substitute doll).
4. `cd spa-pokeuniverse && npx tsc --noEmit -p .`

**Conventions:**
- Task: `*AnimTask_X(run, args)`. Code up to the first `yield` runs at creation, so if the C sets `func` without calling it, start with `yield`. `gAnimVisualTaskCount--` (not waited for): `run.spawn(gen)`.
- Sprite: `init(sprite, run, args, done)` is its creation call; sprites start at the target's picture. `sprite.callback = …`, or `runSprite(sprite, gen, done)` (first step at once; start with `yield` if the C only sets the callback). `sprite.data` not needed: use locals.
- `args` is all of `gBattleAnimArgs` (a copy, safe to read past a command's own, stale values kept as in C): never mutate it, use locals for C's negations. Tasks answer in `run.args[7]` (`[15]` for AnimTask_IsPowerOver99).
- Coordinates: `GetBattlerSpriteCoord(X_2, Y_PIC_OFFSET)` = `run.center(AnimBattler.X)`; `(X, Y)` = `battlerCoords(run, which)`; sides: `flip(run, v)` (attacker is the foe), `flipTarget`, `sideX`; `run.target.foe`.
- Colors: game masks via `selectedPalettes`/`paletteMask`/`paletteOffsets`; a battler's palette at `256 + mon.paletteNum * 16`; `gba.palettes` `blendPalette`, `beginFade`, `faded`, `unfaded`; a tag: `tagPaletteOffset(run, id)` (a new tag passed as an arg needs an entry in `TAG_BY_ID`) or `spritePaletteSlot("ANIM_TAG_X")`.
- Sound: `run.play(se as SoundId, run.pan(p))`; sounds named in a task or a sprite template go in `TASK_SOUNDS`, keyed by its name (and must be in `firered/sounds.json`). Extra art: `ART` + `TASK_ART`, read with `run.art(name)`. Own sprites: `run.createSprite(template, x, y, sub, args)`.
- Move data: `run.movePower`, `run.moveDamage`, `run.turn`, `run.rolloutHit`, `run.weather`, `run.animArg`, `run.customPan` (gAnimCustomPanning). `run.opponent` is the battler across from the attacker (the target may be the attacker itself); `AnimMon` also has `species`, `hidden`, `behindSubstitute`, `dress(art)`.
- **Speeds:** `InitAnimLinearTranslationWithSpeed` is `speedSteps` (`<< 8`); `InitAnimFastLinearTranslationWithSpeed` is `fastSteps`/`fastLine` (`<< 4`). Mixing them runs 16× too slow.
- **General animations:** tasks that pick battlers (`AnimTask_SetAnim*`, `GetBattlersFromArg`) do nothing; `playback.ts` `animateEffect` passes `attackerFoe`, `targetFoe`.
- Check the expansion's C before its moves (M10) use a behavior: it changed some callbacks' args.

**Kit (`behaviors/util.ts`, read its signatures only if needed):** `placeAtAttacker`, `placeAtTarget`, `travel`, `flyTo` (next frame, to a point, then done), `arc`, `linear`, `slide`, `slideAt`, `pixelLine` (TranslateSpriteLinear), `speedSteps`, `fastSteps`, `fastLine`, `backOffScreen`, `lineOffScreen`, `squash`, `resetMonScale`, `PIC_HALF`, `flickerLine`, `circle`, `ellipse`, `growingCircle`, `frames`, `animEnd`, `affineEnd`, `goneAtAnimEnd`, `goneAtAffineEnd`, `spriteOnMon` (AnimSpriteOnMonPos), `toTargetLocation` (TranslateAnimSpriteToTargetMonLocation), `throwProjectile` (AnimThrowProjectile), `cloneMon`, `monSprite`, `rotScale`, `arcTan2Neg`, `seekAnim`, `random`, `sin`, `cos`, `INVISIBLE`. Elsewhere: `monAffine` (effects.ts: an affine table on a battler), `setGrayscale`, `monBlend` (dark.ts), `loadNotePalettes`, `itemFall`, `sliceFlicker`, `MUSIC_NOTES_ANIMS`, `THOUGHT_ANIMS` (effects-1.ts), `GROWING_RING` (water.ts), `clipBelow` (ground.ts), `weatherBallDown`, `twisterParticle`, `BASIC_FIRE`, `WATER_MUD_ORB` (rock.ts), `BUBBLE_AFFINE`, `DROPLET_AFFINE` (poison.ts), `HANDS_AND_FEET`, `BASIC_HIT_SPLAT` (particles.ts). BG art on BG1 + OBJ window: model in `AnimTask_MetallicShine` (dark.ts).

## Known Differences

- **Approximations:**
  - Battlers are sprites: scanline waves sway the whole sprite (Dragon Dance); Dig's clip is a window hiding sprites below a line; under `monbg` a battler's own blended sprite stays opaque (FireRed shows it over a still copy, so no ghost while Roar or Growth scale it).
  - `moveDamage` is the move's power (the damage event comes after the animation): Seismic Toss's level and damage shakes follow power. `rolloutHit` isn't filled: always the first hit's particles. Water Spout's HP quarter comes from `movePower` (always full until the power is sent). Surf's wave blends the whole BG (FireRed widens the blended lines by scanlines). Memento's shadows are black sprite copies that slide and narrow; Spite's shadow a tinted copy that sways; Extrasensory sways the target; Sketch's target is hidden until the drawing ends; Fury Cutter's count is always its first hit; Return's and Frustration's power levels come from `movePower` (friendship isn't sent); Transform pinches the sprite (no mosaic); Acid Armor and Rapid Spin's wipe are sprite stand-ins. `PIC_HALF` (24) stands for `GetBattlerSpriteCoordAttr`'s opaque box (tear drops, question mark, shadows).
  - `monAffine`'s ground offset assumes no Y delta; `AnimTask_MetallicShine` assumes `metal_shine.bin` maps to palette 8; the falling feather's state machine is condensed (`flying.ts`); cry modes aren't rendered: Growl, Roar, Hyper Voice (the normal cry twice) and Howl (once) use the normal cry.
  - Snatch's second sprite mirrors the attacker's picture (the C draws its other side). Castform's change plays with no form art. Terrain is always the building (Secret Power, Camouflage). With BATTLE SCENE off no doll is shown. A Substitute passed by Baton Pass shows no doll.
- **Testing in the browser pane:** game at `localhost:8080/play`; keys Z = A, X = B, Enter = START (dispatch keydown/keyup on window and document, held ~80 ms). Reloading drops a battle (D22): face the trainer above, A, YES. A hidden pane gets no frames: wrap `requestAnimationFrame` with `setTimeout(16)` while `document.hidden`. To watch an animation, record the canvas each frame (crop the centered 3:2 area to 240×160) and show chosen frames as an overlay grid. **Cheapest way to see every move:** a throwaway build (never committed; `docker cp` into the SPA container, then rebuild it clean) with a one-line hook at the top of `animateMove` playing a list of moves or `B_ANIM_*` names from `window` in one turn, marking each one's first frame; show 6 frames per move, the most different from its first (12 moves per screenshot). For real events, override the party's moves in `team()` and the trainer's team temporarily, and never finish that battle (a win saves its outcome): reload to drop it (D22). Wait for the menu by a white pixel in recorded frames, not by time. The running image serves a bundle: rebuild it (`docker compose up --build -d spa-pokeuniverse`) and clear the service worker in the pane.
- **Gotchas:** the expansion changed some callbacks' args: check both C files before expansion moves use a behavior. Power Herb (M12) skips the charge: Showdown logs `-prepare` then `-anim` (not translated), so it shows the charge and no strike anim yet. A semi-invulnerable user shown again by a resume redraw isn't hidden (rare). The orb's tint blends its whole tag palette. `run.center()` is the sprite's position (fixed spot after a faint). BG1 and palette 8 are the animations' (BG_ANIM 1); the scene doesn't use them.
